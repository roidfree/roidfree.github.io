Just got news - we’re officially working with Rylie Green’s Green Group Research Group Lab members! 

This was a huge step for the project. The Green Group’s expertise in **conductive polymers and bioelectronic materials** is exactly we were eyeing it up from the beginning.

Truth is, we don’t have hands-on experience with the materials side of things—how to actually _make_ these advanced, skin-like conductors. That’s why we’re beyond grateful to have brilliant folks like **Josef** jumping in to support us.
